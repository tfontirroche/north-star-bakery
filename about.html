Our Story

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood bakery focused on bringing fresh, handmade baked goods to the local community. From everyday breads and pastries to treats for special occasions, our goal is to create a warm and welcoming bakery experience for everyone who visits.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe great baked goods begin with thoughtfully selected ingredients. Our approach focuses on quality, freshness, and care in every item we prepare for our community.

Meet Our Team

Our bakery team works together to prepare fresh breads, pastries, and cakes with care. From preparing dough to putting the finishing touches on each item, our staff is dedicated to creating baked goods our community can enjoy.

Visit Our Bakery

We welcome our neighbors, commuters, families, and visitors to stop by and enjoy our freshly baked selections.
